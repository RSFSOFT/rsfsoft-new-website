Our Process

How We Deliver Your WordPress Site

A smooth, transparent process that keeps you in the loop at every stage — from first call to final handover.

1
Discovery Call
We start with a free consultation to understand your business, goals and what you need from your WordPress site. We ask the right questions, listen carefully and document everything. This forms the foundation of your project brief and ensures we build exactly what you need, first time.
2
Design & Approve
Our designers create stunning Figma mockups showing every key page of your site. You review, give feedback and we refine until you love it. Only when you've approved the design in full do we move to development — guaranteeing no nasty surprises when you see the finished website.
3
Build & Test
Our WordPress experts code your theme from scratch, set up your plugins, configure WooCommerce (if applicable) and build out all pages on a private staging environment. We run comprehensive QA testing across 20+ devices and browsers, fix every issue found and share staging access for your review.
4
Launch & Train
We migrate your approved site to the live server with zero downtime, configure SSL, set up caching, submit your sitemap to Google and monitor performance. Then we provide full WordPress training so you can confidently manage your site, along with detailed documentation and ongoing support access.
