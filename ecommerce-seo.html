E-Commerce SEO Questions Answered

E-commerce SEO focuses on optimizing product pages, category pages, and handling technical challenges unique to large product catalogs like faceted navigation, duplicate content from product variants, and canonicalization. It requires specialized knowledge of each platform's architecture and how Google crawls and indexes large numbers of product URLs — expertise that generic SEO agencies typically lack.
Most clients see significant ranking improvements within 3–6 months, with traffic and sales growth continuing to compound month-over-month with ongoing optimization. E-commerce SEO is a long-term investment — the earlier you start, the greater the compounding advantage you build over competitors who delay.
We work across all major platforms — Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento and custom e-commerce builds. We have specialist experience with each platform's unique SEO requirements, common technical pitfalls and the most effective optimization approaches.
