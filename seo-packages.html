What's Included

Every Package Includes These Core Services

No matter which plan you choose, these six fundamentals are always included — because great SEO starts with a strong foundation.

Keyword Research & Strategy
In-depth keyword discovery using premium tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz). We identify high-intent, achievable keywords mapped to your buyer journey — targeting terms your customers actually use when they're ready to buy.
On-Page Optimisation
Every page optimised for your target keywords: meta titles, descriptions, H1–H6 hierarchy, image alt text, internal linking, URL structure and keyword density. Google-friendly without sacrificing readability.
Technical SEO
Comprehensive technical health checks: Core Web Vitals, page speed optimisation, crawl error fixes, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, schema markup, HTTPS security, mobile responsiveness and canonicalisation.
Link Building
White-hat outreach campaigns to secure editorial backlinks from real, relevant, high-authority websites. Every link is manually vetted. No link farms. No PBNs. Just genuine authority signals that move your rankings.
Content Creation
SEO-optimised blog posts, landing pages and resource content written by UK-based copywriters. Every piece is keyword-researched, E-E-A-T compliant and designed to rank, engage and convert your target audience.
Monthly Reporting
Beautifully clear monthly reports showing keyword ranking movements, organic traffic trends, backlinks earned, content published and next month's strategy. Full transparency — you always know exactly what we're doing and why.
