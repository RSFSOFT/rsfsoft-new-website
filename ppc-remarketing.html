Standard Remarketing
Show ads to people who previously visited your site. Even those who used your mobile app can see the ads. Many people visit a site but don't buy — remarketing reconnects with those visitors and gives you a second chance to convert them into customers.
Dynamic Remarketing
Products and services people viewed on your website are included in the ads retargeting them. This technique delivers highly targeted, personalised ads showing exactly what the visitor was interested in — at exactly the right moment in their buying journey.

Remarketing Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about PPC remarketing campaigns.

Remarketing and retargeting are essentially the same concept. Google uses the term 'remarketing'; other platforms use 'retargeting'. Both involve showing ads to people who previously visited your website or interacted with your brand.
We install a Google Ads tracking tag on your website that adds visitors to remarketing audience lists. These lists are then used to show your ads specifically to those users as they browse other websites or use apps.
Google allows remarketing audience windows of up to 540 days. We typically recommend 30–90 day windows depending on your product, service and typical sales cycle length.
