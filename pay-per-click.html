What Is PPC Advertising?

PPC (Pay Per Click) is a form of digital advertising where you pay only when someone clicks your ad. Your ads appear in sponsored positions on search engines like Google, or across social media platforms like Facebook and Instagram. Unlike SEO, PPC delivers immediate visibility — your ads can appear within hours of going live.

FAQ

Common PPC Questions

What is the minimum budget for PPC? +
We recommend a minimum ad spend of £500/month to gather meaningful data and drive results. Our management fee is separate and based on your ad spend volume. We work with all budget sizes.
How quickly do PPC ads show results? +
Unlike SEO, PPC ads can appear and drive traffic within hours of launch. However, it typically takes 2–4 weeks of optimisation to reach peak performance as we gather data and refine targeting.
Which PPC platform is best for my business? +
It depends on your target audience and goals. Google Search Ads work best for capturing existing demand. Facebook/Instagram Ads are great for building awareness and reaching specific demographics. We'll advise the best mix during your free audit.
Do you guarantee PPC results? +
No ethical agency can guarantee specific results — PPC performance depends on your market, competition and budget. However, we guarantee to maximise your ROI using proven strategies, continuous optimisation and full transparent reporting.
