Our Process

From Idea to Live in 4 Steps

A proven, transparent process refined over 750+ project deliveries. You'll know exactly what's happening at every stage and why.

1
Discovery
We deep-dive into your business goals, target audience, competitors and requirements. A thorough discovery session ensures we build exactly what you need — no guesswork. We document everything in a clear project brief you approve before a single line of code is written.
2
Design
Our UI/UX designers craft stunning, conversion-optimised wireframes and high-fidelity mockups in Figma. You'll see exactly how your website will look before development begins. We iterate on your feedback until every pixel is perfect and you're 100% happy.
3
Development
Our expert developers bring the approved design to life using clean, semantic, standards-compliant code. We build mobile-first, performance-first and SEO-first. You get regular progress updates and staging environment access throughout, with rigorous testing before any deployment.
4
Launch
We handle the full deployment, DNS configuration, SSL setup, performance optimisation and post-launch monitoring. Your site goes live with zero downtime. We include full handover documentation, CMS training and 30 days of free post-launch support so you're set up for long-term success.
