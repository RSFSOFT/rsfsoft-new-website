Campaign Execution & Strategy

With ride-hailing apps dominating the market, Taxi Bolton (taxibolton.com) needed a way for their local dispatch service to compete by capturing commuters directly from search. We engineered a mobile-first website featuring direct click-to-call links and an intuitive taxi fare calculator that estimates ride prices instantly. We implemented schema markups for local transportation services to help search engines index their services correctly.

Our local SEO strategy involved establishing strong authority in Bolton and surrounding areas. We targeted keywords with transactional intent, such as 'airport taxi Bolton', 'Bolton taxi numbers', and 'cheap taxi near me'. We set up automated email and SMS request funnels for passengers to leave Google Maps reviews immediately after their journeys.

As a result of our persistent optimization since 2020, Taxi Bolton now dominates search engine results for local airport transfers and daily commuting queries in Bolton, bringing in over 400 5-star reviews and showing a 220% growth in online bookings.
