The Challenge

Mirza Couture had operated as a beloved women's fashion boutique in Lahore for 14 years. Owner Fatima Mirza had built a genuinely loyal local customer base through word-of-mouth and the quality of her designs — particularly her Eid and wedding collections. But she had a clear ambition: to expand nationally to Karachi and Islamabad without the capital expense of opening physical stores.

The digital reality was painful. Their Instagram page had 2,100 followers with an average engagement rate of 0.6% (industry average: 3.5%). Their Facebook page had 1,300 followers. They had no website — every single online order was processed via WhatsApp message, which meant Fatima was personally managing customer conversations, size queries, payment confirmations and delivery tracking for every order. This worked at 10 orders per month. It was completely unscalable at 100.

Our Strategy

The 10-Month Plan

1

🛍️ Shopify Store Launch (Month 1–2)

Built a premium custom Shopify store in 5 weeks. Loaded with 340 products across categories (pret, formal, wedding, Eid collections). Beautiful product photography guidelines shared with Fatima's team. Integrated with JazzCash and EasyPaisa for Pakistan-native payments, plus bank transfer options. WhatsApp Business chat widget for post-purchase support. Store launched officially in Week 6 — first online order within 3 hours of going live.

2

🎨 Brand Visual Identity System (Month 1)

Created a complete brand visual identity: colour palette (dusty rose, ivory, deep plum), custom Instagram grid templates, font pairing, photography style guide (flat-lay with rose gold props, model shots on neutral backgrounds, Lahore rooftop lifestyle shots). Provided Fatima's team with 60 ready-to-use templates in Canva. From Month 2, every post was immediately identifiable as Mirza Couture.

3

📅 Content Calendar — 3 Posts/Day (Month 2 onwards)

Implemented a strict content calendar across Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. 3 posts per day: morning product feature, afternoon lifestyle/behind-the-scenes, evening engagement post (polls, questions, "which one?" comparisons). TikTok videos showing fabric quality, packaging process, and seasonal lookbooks. Instagram Reels using trending audio boosted reach by 340% compared to static posts.

4

🤝 Micro-Influencer Strategy (Month 2–10)

Partnered with 34 carefully selected Pakistani fashion influencers — all with 50K–500K followers (avoiding mega-influencers whose audiences are passive). Focus on fashion, lifestyle and wedding content creators based in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad. Each influencer received a custom clothing set and an affiliate discount code tracked back to them. Average campaign ROI across all 34 partnerships: 6.2x. Best single influencer campaign: PKR 340,000 in attributed sales from one Eid post.

5

💰 Meta Ads with Lookalike Audiences (Month 3 onwards)

Built Meta ad campaigns using purchaser lookalike audiences from Shopify customer data. Targeted women aged 20–45 in Pakistan's top 8 cities. Creative strategy: short video clips of fabric quality and unboxing experience performed 3x better than product flat-lays. Retargeting ads to website visitors with "Limited Stock" urgency achieved 4.8x ROAS. Eid campaign budget: PKR 180,000 — generated PKR 860,000 in attributed revenue.

Key Lessons From This Campaign

The decision to launch the Shopify store before focusing on social growth was the right sequence. Many brands invest months building a social following before they have anywhere to send their audience to buy. By having a beautiful, functional store live from Month 2, every piece of content we created was immediately monetisable — and the data from real purchases helped us build better ad audiences faster.

The micro-influencer strategy outperformed our expectations significantly. In Pakistani fashion, followers of a 150K lifestyle influencer trust her product recommendations far more than they trust a billboard — because she feels like a friend. The key was choosing influencers whose aesthetic matched Mirza Couture's — not just going for the biggest follower counts. Authenticity drove conversion. Every partnership was measured by attributed Shopify revenue, not just reach or impressions.
