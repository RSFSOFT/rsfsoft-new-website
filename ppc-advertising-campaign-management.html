Advanced Strategies

Remarketing, Dynamic Ads & Beyond

PPC Remarketing

If you've heard about remarketing for the first time, it's a service that gives you the flexibility to show ads to people who visited your website in the past. Even those who use your mobile app can see the ads. Many people visit a site but don't buy anything on the first visit — remarketing gives you the power to reconnect with those warm visitors and bring them back to convert.

Dynamic Remarketing

Dynamic remarketing takes personalisation to the next level — ads include the exact products and services people viewed on your website, retargeting them with highly personalised content at exactly the right moment. The result is much better conversion rates as you deliver highly targeted ads to exactly the right customer at the right time, dramatically reducing your cost per acquisition.
