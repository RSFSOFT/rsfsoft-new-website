FAQ

Display Advertising Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before launching your display advertising campaign.

Display advertising shows visual banner and image ads across millions of websites in advertising networks. Unlike search ads that appear when someone searches, display ads appear while people are browsing other websites — great for building brand awareness.
We target by interest categories, purchase intent, demographics, geography, device type and contextual relevance (placing ads on websites related to your products or services). This ensures your ads reach the most relevant users.
We recommend a minimum of £500/month for display campaigns to gather meaningful performance data. Display ads are generally more cost-effective per impression than search ads, making them ideal for brand awareness campaigns.
