Types of Penalties

Manual & Algorithmic Penalties

Understanding the type of penalty is the first step to recovery. We handle both.

Manual Penalties
Applied by Google human reviewers. Visible in Google Search Console under Manual Actions. Triggered by link schemes, thin content, cloaking, structured data abuse and other guideline violations.

Recovery involves fixing the underlying issues and submitting a formal Reconsideration Request to Google. Our team writes these to professional standards — clear, evidenced and persuasive.
Algorithmic Penalties
Triggered by Google algorithm updates — Panda (content quality), Penguin (backlinks), Helpful Content, Core Updates and others. Show as sudden traffic drops without any Search Console notification.

There is no reconsideration — recovery means fixing the root causes and waiting for Google to re-crawl and re-evaluate your site. We diagnose the specific update and build an action plan.
