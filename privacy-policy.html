Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: June 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our website or services, including: your name, email address, phone number, and business details submitted via contact and quote forms. We also automatically collect technical data such as IP addresses, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and session duration through cookies and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to: respond to your enquiries and provide the services you've requested; process transactions and manage your account; send you marketing communications about our services (you can opt out at any time); improve our website performance and user experience; comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us understand how visitors interact with our website. You can control cookie settings through your browser, but disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

4. Sharing Your Information

RSF Soft does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers who assist in delivering our services (e.g. hosting, analytics, email delivery), all of whom are required to maintain appropriate data protection standards. We may also disclose information when required by law or legal processes.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations); withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@rsfsoft.com.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.
